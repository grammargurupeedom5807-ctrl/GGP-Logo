🚀 Step 2: Pay via UPI App
You can copy the UPI ID into your UPI Payment App. Tap anywhere on the UPI ID box above and copy it. Then tap on the Pay via UPI App button. You can paste the UPI ID in the search box as shown below, verify the banking name, and type the amount.
🔵 In Google Pay (GPay)
  1. Open Google Pay.
  2. On the home screen, tap Pay UPI ID or number.
  3. Long-press inside the search bar, tap Paste, and click Continue.
  4. Enter the package amount and pay!
举 In PhonePe
  1. Open PhonePe.
  2. In the "Transfer Money" section, tap To Bank/UPI ID.
  3. Tap the UPI ID tab at the top of the screen.
  4. Tap the search bar (or the + plus icon at bottom right).
  5. Long-press, tap Paste, click Verify, and save to pay!
🟢 In Paytm
  1. Open Paytm.
  2. On the home screen, tap To Bank A/c or UPI ID.
  3. Tap Enter UPI ID of any app.
  4. Long-press inside the input box, tap Paste, and hit Proceed to pay!
